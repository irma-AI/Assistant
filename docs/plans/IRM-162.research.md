# IRM-162 research: Material Design 3 and Android quality guidelines

Read on 2026-10-25. Apple's HIG notes are in `IRM-160.research.md`.

## Material Design 3 (m3.material.io)

- Accessibility, Structure: touch targets at least 48×48 dp, about 9 mm; targets 8 dp apart; notes that iOS recommends 44×44.
- Accessibility, Elements: label interactive icons and meaningful images by purpose; don't put the role in the label; hide decorative images.
- Gestures: tap, double tap, long press, scroll, swipe, drag, pinch; predictive back on Android.
- Dialogs: only for high-importance prompts; headline a clear statement or question, no "Are you sure?"; at most two actions, confirming action at the trailing edge, dismissive before it; confirming action names the result; full-screen dialog asks before discarding unsaved changes; field errors inline, general errors in a dialog.
- Snackbar: one at a time, at the bottom, at most one action such as Undo; on web an auto-dismissing snackbar is inaccessible, so repeat the information inline or make it actionable.
- Navigation bar: three to five destinations, icon plus label, always visible, fixed positions; fewer than three, use tabs.
- Window size classes: compact, medium and expanded breakpoints (600 and 840 dp).
- Text fields: every field has a label that stays visible.
- Progress indicators: determinate when progress is known, indeterminate otherwise, switching when known.
- Motion: spring-based tokens; no rule beyond HIG's is needed for one design.

## Android developers (developer.android.com)

- Core app quality: Touch_Target_Size 48 dp; Visual_Contrast 4.5:1 and 3:1; light and dark themes; Back_Button_Nav (standard back, no custom back prompts); Back_Gesture_Nav; State_Preservation; Orientation_Transitions; App_Startup_Time (progress after 2 s); Rendering_Performance (60 fps); Runtime_Permissions at use, Minimize_Permissions; Notification_Quality (relevant, no promotion, channels, groups).
- Edge-to-edge: enforced on Android 15+ once the app targets SDK 35; this app targets 36 (`android/variables.gradle`). Handle system bar, cutout and system gesture insets.
- Predictive back: system back animations apply to apps that opt in.
- Android 14: non-linear font scaling up to 200 %.
- Request runtime permissions: ask in context; show a rationale when the system says to; degrade gracefully on denial.
- Notification runtime permission: on Android 13+ notifications are off until POST_NOTIFICATIONS is granted.
