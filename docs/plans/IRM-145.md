# IRM-145: Create and send invoices (B2B and B2C)

See the Linear issue (description plus its Decisions, which are the spec).

## Progress
- Migration 0015 (invoices, lines, series counter, `invoices_guard`, `invoice_rates`, `ocr_reference`), `src/api/invoices.ts`, the `invoices.*.server.ts` files, the PDF writer (`invoicePdf.server.ts`, no dependency) and the routes under `/api/v1/orgs/$orgId/invoices/`.
- Proof for every criterion: `pnpm exec vitest run src/server/invoices` (12 tests, real PostgreSQL).

## Decisions
- API only, like IRM-151: the invoice pages (with the trust notes: recipient, channel, total and due date before sending) are a follow-up UI issue.
- ROT/RUT is IRM-170 and VAT codes are IRM-163. Until IRM-163 lands a line carries one of `25`, `12`, `6`, `0` or `rc` (reverse charge), mapped to fixed BAS accounts.

- A sent invoice carries copies of the buyer and seller (jsonb); the PDF is a retained document (downloaded through `/documents/:id`), linked to the booking voucher under the invoice method and given `retain_until` directly under the cash method.
- Email goes out after the commit: a failed mail leaves the invoice sent, `POST .../email` retries. Mail is HostUp SMTP through the existing `SMTP_URL`/`MAIL_FROM`; the owner's mailbox and secret (IRM-54) are still needed before it sends in production.
- Sent invoices keep `customer_id` only as a link: deleting the customer sets it to null and the guard allows exactly that change.
