# IRM-149 Payment watch: notify when invoices are paid or overdue

Description: see the issue in Linear (shaped 2026-10-25). Decisions 1-6 and the five acceptance criteria are in `IRM-149.criteria.json`. Summary: members with `invoices.manage` are told when an invoice is paid, partly paid, overpaid, due tomorrow, or overdue (first day, then weekly). Incoming bank payments are matched to open invoices in four passes (0.98, 0.92, 0.85, 0.70; nothing below 0.70), suggested, and booked only when a person confirms. Customer reminders (IRM-174) and push (IRM-173) are out of scope.

## Decisions
- New table `invoice_payments` (invoice, bank transaction, status suggested/confirmed/rejected, pass, confidence, covers_ore, settlement voucher); `invoice_watch` holds one row per notice sent, so a job retry never repeats an event.
- Open amount = invoice total − confirmed payments − sent credit notes. Computed in SQL, not stored.
- Passes are a pure function `src/api/paymentMatch.ts`. The invoice stores no customer giro, so pass 2 (the "giro" signal) is the exact amount plus the invoice number in the bank's reference text.
- Pass 1 (OCR) suggests whatever amount arrived; `covers_ore` is min(open, payment), the rest is flagged overpaid.
- Settlement voucher on confirm, dated by the payment: invoice method, debit 1930 and credit 1510; cash method, debit 1930 and credit revenue and output VAT in the invoice's proportions (largest remainder per payment).
- Events fire when a person confirms (paid / partly paid / overpaid); the job `invoice-watch` (hourly) creates due-tomorrow and overdue events. No event for a mere suggestion.
- Unmatched incoming payments are listed ("needs a person"); linking one by hand is not built here.
