# IRM-155: Document storage: receipts, invoice PDFs and attachments, kept 7 years

Full issue text (questions and Decisions 1-9) is in Linear.

## Progress
- Scope: `documents` table (org-scoped RLS, SHA-256, retention and immutability triggers), a storage interface with a local-volume backend (`STORAGE_DIR`), and `/api/v1/orgs/$orgId/documents` upload, list and streamed download.
- Not in this PR: linking a document to a posted entry (IRM-151 sets `retain_until`), HEIC JPEG previews, the volume mount in Dokploy and the off-site copy (IRM-98), native photo upload.

## Decisions
- Unlinked documents (`retain_until` null) are drafts: deletable in SQL, but the app never deletes. Setting `retain_until` is the link; after that every update is rejected and delete is blocked until that date.
- Content columns (hash, size, key, type, name) are immutable always.
- The trigger's block is not written to the audit log: the raising statement rolls the insert back; revisit with IRM-151.
- Storage keys are `<org>/<document id>`; the filename lives only in the database.
