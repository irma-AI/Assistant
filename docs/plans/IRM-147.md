## Decisions
- Receipts are rows over `documents` (one per document); amount, date, supplier are entered by hand until IRM-171 adds AI.
- Match passes are a pure function in `src/api/receiptMatch.ts` (0.98, 0.92, 0.85, 0.70; nothing below 0.70). One suggestion per receipt, the best one.
- Split matches are manual: a confirm is allowed while the confirmed sums on both sides stay within the amounts. Suggestions are 1:1 only.
- A correction (manual match to another transaction) stores the supplier name against the bank text, so pass 4 finds it next time.
- Account suggestion: from earlier vouchers whose underlag had the same supplier. No VAT code suggestion: the app has no VAT codes yet (add with the VAT work).
- Confirm, reject and correct need `bookkeeping.manage`; every member may upload receipts.
