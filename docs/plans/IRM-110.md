## Decisions

- The BankID-seen IP is compared only on a same-device login. In a QR login it is the scanning phone's IP, so comparing would refuse most real logins; `risk` still applies to both.
- Design is as in IRM-89's plan, with `pollLogin` instead of `status`. The attempt row holds no `same_device` column; a null `nonce` means a QR login.
- QR data is offered for 30 seconds; BankID ends the order itself and a login being signed is never cancelled by us.
- Attempts older than 30 minutes are swept every 15 minutes, so none lives an hour.
