# IRM-161: BankID QR login stops after 30 seconds; BankID requires at least 5 minutes and an offer of more time

## Problem

`src/server/login.server.ts` (around line 246) stops offering QR data 30 seconds after the order starts. The test "after 30 seconds no QR data is offered…" in `login.server.test.ts` locks that in. Someone who is slow to find their phone, or who uses assistive technology, gets no QR code after half a minute.

## What BankID requires

From BankID's accessibility requirements for QR login. These follow from WCAG 2.x AA / EN 301 549, which lag (2023:254) makes mandatory for this kind of service:

* The QR code is shown for **at least 5 minutes** in total.
* It is regenerated **at least 10 times**, so a new order is started when the old one runs out.
* When **20 seconds or less** remain, the user is offered more time. The user chooses; it doesn't happen silently.
* The QR can be clicked or tapped to **full screen** (already done in `-BankIdPanel.tsx`).
* It has a screen-reader text, for example "QR-kod, skanna på armlängds avstånd. Tryck för helskärm." Today the alt text is "QR-kod för BankID-inloggning".

## Done when

* A QR login stays scannable for at least 5 minutes, across new orders where needed. Each new order cancels the old one.
* With 20 seconds or less left, the panel offers "Mer tid" and continuing works.
* The QR image's accessible name matches BankID's recommended wording.
* The 30-second test is replaced by tests for the new timing, and an e2e test covers the extend flow with the fake BankID.
* Throttling from IRM-130 still holds: extending must not let one client start unlimited orders.

Check BankID's current developer docs for the exact numbers before implementing.

## Progress

- Read BankID's current docs on 2026-10-25: developers.bankid.com/getting-started/frontend/qr-code (Countdown timer and extend button) and bankid.com/en/accessibility/accessible-qr-codes. An order lasts 30 seconds; show the QR about 5 minutes by placing new orders; show "extend" when 30 seconds remain and reset to 5 minutes; read the countdown aloud at 5–1 minutes and 50–10 seconds; space bar or button extends; focus returns to the QR button.
- Migration `0007_login_qr_window.sql` adds `order_at` and `qr_until`.
- Server: `qrStep` replaces an unscanned order 25 seconds old (or one BankID ended unscanned) while QR time remains, cancels the old one, and ends the attempt with RFA17 when time runs out. `extendLogin` (`POST /api/v1/auth/bankid/extend`) resets to 5 minutes, capped at 30 minutes after the attempt started. Tests: `pnpm exec vitest run src/server/login.qr.test.ts src/server/login.server.test.ts` (21 passed).
- UI: `-QrCode.tsx` with BankID's accessible name, visible countdown, aria-live key-point announcements, "Mer tid" from 30 seconds, space bar, focus back to the QR. Tests: `pnpm exec vitest run src/routes/app/login/`; e2e `e2e/login.spec.ts` passed locally in Chromium (9 passed); CI runs WebKit.

## Decisions

- "Mer tid" shows at 30 seconds left, as BankID's developer guide says, which satisfies the issue's "20 seconds or less".
- Orders per attempt are bounded: one every 25 seconds at most, and no QR time past 30 minutes after the attempt started, which is also when the sweep deletes it; so at most 72 orders per attempt. Throttling `start` itself stays with IRM-130.
- Only an order nobody has scanned is replaced or ended (`outstandingTransaction` pending, or `startFailed`); an order being signed always runs to its end.
- Wider class of bug: a timer copied from a vendor's protocol limit (BankID's 30-second order) became the user's limit. The user-facing time now has its own column.
