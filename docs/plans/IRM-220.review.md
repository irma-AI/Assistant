**Owner?** `ja` means the fix changes look or words (an owner question). `nej` means it only brings the screen in line with a DESIGN.md or PRODUCT.md rule. Rules are named as `DESIGN.md:<line>`, by section, or by file.

**Marks:** *kept* = a first-review finding the redo confirmed; *new* = added by the procedures; *overturned* = the redo shows the first review was wrong or incomplete.
