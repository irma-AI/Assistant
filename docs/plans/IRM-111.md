## Decisions
- Changing or removing a member also needs the permissions of the member's current role, so `members.manage` alone cannot demote an Ägare.
- Of two Ägare demoting each other at once, the loser gets 403 (no longer Ägare), not 409; one Ägare remains either way.
- Account deletion (IRM-92, on main) goes through `deleteUser`: it locks the user's organisations, refuses the only Ägare, and removes memberships before the user. The hand-over link is IRM-126.
- Audited: organisation, role and membership changes, plus every 403 and non-member 404.
